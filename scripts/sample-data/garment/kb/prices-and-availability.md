# Prices, discounts and availability

## Why a price on Ethnic Threads can differ from the store

The prices on Ethnic Threads are a snapshot. A program (the scraper) visits Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, reads each listing, and saves the price it saw at that moment. Stores change prices, run flash sales and end offers at any time, so the price on the store can be different from the price shown here. The store's price is always the one you pay.

## How fresh the catalogue is

When the scraper is running it refreshes the catalogue in cycles, one after another, through the day. Each listing remembers when it was last scraped. If the scraper has been stopped or is failing, the catalogue is not refreshed and prices go out of date. The support team is alerted automatically when the catalogue has not been refreshed for two days.

## Discount percentages

The discount shown is worked out from the two prices the store displayed when the listing was scraped: the current price and the original price. If the store showed no original price, no discount is shown.

## Out of stock items

Stock is also a snapshot. An item shown as available may have sold out at the store since the last scrape, and an item shown as sold out may be back. Open the listing on the store to see the current stock.

## Ratings

Ratings are the ones the store showed when the listing was scraped. Ethnic Threads does not collect its own reviews.
