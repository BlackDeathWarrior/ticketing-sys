# WhatsApp

## Asking about your orders on WhatsApp

To ask about your orders on WhatsApp, add your WhatsApp number to your account first. Sign in on the website and open Account. Enter your WhatsApp number with its country code, then type the code that arrives on WhatsApp. Once the number is added, you can ask about your orders by writing to us on WhatsApp.

## Changing the number

You can change the number the same way: open Account, enter the new WhatsApp number with its country code and type the code that arrives on WhatsApp.

## Signing in with WhatsApp

Once a number has been added to your account, you can also sign in on the website with a code sent to that number on WhatsApp.
