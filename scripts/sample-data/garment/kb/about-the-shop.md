# About Ethnic Threads

## What Ethnic Threads is

Ethnic Threads is an online shop for Indian ethnic wear: kurtas, kurta sets, sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, Nehru jackets and more, for women and men. You browse the catalogue, add items to your cart and check out on the site.

## A demonstration shop

Ethnic Threads is a demonstration shop. Orders move through the same steps as in a real shop, but no money is taken and nothing is shipped. No card or UPI details are ever asked for.

## Accounts

Anyone can browse and fill a cart. To check out you need an account: your name, your email address and a password of at least 8 characters. Your orders and your requests to support are kept under your account, under "Your Orders" and "Help".

## Sizes and quantities

Each item is offered in sizes S, M, L, XL, XXL and Free size. You can order up to 5 of each item, and up to 20 different items in one order.
