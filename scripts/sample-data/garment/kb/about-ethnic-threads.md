# About Ethnic Threads

## What Ethnic Threads is

Ethnic Threads is a catalogue of Indian ethnic wear. It collects listings for kurtas, sarees, lehengas, sherwanis and similar clothing from three online stores (Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra) and shows them in one place, so you can compare prices and discounts without visiting each store.

## Ethnic Threads does not sell anything

Ethnic Threads is not a shop. You cannot place an order, pay, or check out on Ethnic Threads. Each listing has a "Buy at" button that opens the product on the store that sells it. You buy from that store, with that store's checkout.

## Orders, delivery, returns and refunds

Because you buy from the store and not from Ethnic Threads, the store handles everything after the click: the order, the payment, delivery, returns, exchanges and refunds. Ethnic Threads has no record of your orders and cannot track, cancel, return or refund them. For any of these, contact the store you bought from (Amazon, Flipkart or Myntra) through your account there.

## What support can help with

Ethnic Threads support can help with the site itself: a listing that shows a wrong price, a product that is out of stock, a link that does not open, wrong product details, a problem with search or filters, and suggestions for the site.
