# Delivery

## Delivery options and charges

Standard delivery takes about 4 days and costs ₹49. It is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Express delivery takes about 2 days and costs ₹99 on every order.

## Following your order

Open the order under "Your Orders". It shows the five steps an order goes through, and when each was reached: Order placed, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered. Once the order has shipped, the page also shows the carrier and the tracking number.

## Who delivers

Orders are delivered by our carrier, SwiftShip. The tracking number starts with "SW".

## If your order is delayed

If the carrier is running late, your order page says "Delayed with the carrier". You do not need to do anything: the order moves again as soon as the carrier does, and the page updates by itself. If it is urgent, choose "Get help with this order" on the order page.

## Changing the delivery address

The address cannot be changed after the order is placed. If the order has not shipped yet, cancel it and order again with the right address.
