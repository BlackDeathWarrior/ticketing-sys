# Getting help

## About an order

Open the order under "Your Orders" and choose "Get help with this order". Pick what it is about and write your question. Support receives the order together with your question, so you do not need to copy its details.

## About anything else

Use "Write to us" under "Help" for questions about a product, a problem with the site, or feedback.

## Following a request

"Help" in the menu lists your requests to support. Open one to read the replies, write back, and rate the answer once it is solved.

## Chat

The chat button at the bottom right is on every page. When you are signed in, the assistant can look up your own orders, cancel an order that has not shipped, and ask a supervisor to approve a refund. It can never see another customer's orders. If you are not signed in, it can answer general questions only: sign in first for anything about an order.
