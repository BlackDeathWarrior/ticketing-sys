# Using the site

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box at the top to search by product name or brand, for example "silk saree" or "Biba kurta". The filters on the left narrow the results by gender, store (source), brand, fabric and colour, and "Sort By" orders them by price, rating or discount. Filters and your search are remembered in your browser for the next visit.

## Accounts

Registering on Ethnic Threads creates an account that is kept in your own browser only. It is not stored on a server, so it does not follow you to another device or browser, and clearing your browser data removes it. There is no password reset: register again if the account is gone.

## Contacting support

"Write to Us" in the menu opens the contact form for suggestions, bug reports and general feedback. The chat button at the bottom right of every page starts a conversation with support. Both give you a reference you can use to follow up.

## Is Ethnic Threads free?

Yes. Browsing and comparing listings on Ethnic Threads is free. You pay only the store, and only if you buy something there.
