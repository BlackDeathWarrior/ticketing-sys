# Reporting a problem with a listing

## How to report

Open the product and choose "Report a problem with this listing" under the buy button. Pick what is wrong (wrong price, out of stock, broken link, wrong details or something else), add a short note, and send. You get a reference number and a link to follow the report.

## What happens next

The report goes to the catalogue team together with the listing it is about, so you do not need to copy the product name or link. The team checks the listing against the store. A wrong price or stock status is usually corrected by scraping the store again. A listing that no longer exists on the store is not removed automatically: the team takes it out of the catalogue.

## Following your report

"My Requests" in the menu lists the messages and reports sent from your browser. Open one to see the replies, add more details, or rate the answer once it is solved.

## A link that does not open

If "Buy at" opens an error page on the store, the store has probably removed or renamed the product. Report the listing as a broken link so the team can remove it here too.
