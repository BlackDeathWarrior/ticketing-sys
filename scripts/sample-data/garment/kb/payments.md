# Payments

## How you can pay

You can pay by cash on delivery, by UPI or by card. With cash on delivery you pay when the order arrives. With UPI or card the order is paid when you place it.

## If a payment fails

If a payment by UPI or card does not go through, the order is not placed and you are not charged. Try again a little later, or choose cash on delivery, which keeps working when online payments have a problem.

## Being charged twice

An order is paid once. If you believe you were charged twice, choose "Get help with this order" on the order page and pick "Payment problem". A refund has to be approved by a supervisor before it is issued.

## Is my payment information safe?

Ethnic Threads is a demonstration shop: it never asks for card numbers, UPI ids or bank details, and no money is taken.
