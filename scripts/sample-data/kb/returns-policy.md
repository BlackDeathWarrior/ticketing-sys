# Returns and refunds policy

This is the fictional returns policy of the Demo Store, written for TMS demos and tests.

## Returning an item

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, with their original packaging and tags. Start a return from the Orders page in your account, or ask support and quote your order number.

We email a prepaid return label within one business day. Drop the parcel at any partner drop-off point; keep the receipt until your refund arrives.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, personalised items, opened bike helmets and consumables such as chain oil cannot be returned, unless they arrived damaged or faulty.

## Refund timing

Once the warehouse receives your return, we inspect it within 3 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method:

- Cards: 5 to 7 business days after approval.
- UPI and wallets: 1 to 2 business days after approval.
- Store credit: immediately after approval.

If your refund has not arrived after those times, contact support with your order number and the return tracking number.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, including the original delivery fee, and you do not need to pay for the return.

## Exchanges

To exchange a size or colour, return the item for a refund and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the new item.
