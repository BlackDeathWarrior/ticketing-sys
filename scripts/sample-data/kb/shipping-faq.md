# Shipping and delivery FAQ

Fictional delivery information for the Demo Store, used in TMS demos and tests.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and 5 to 8 business days elsewhere. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available for orders placed before 2 pm.

## How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is free on orders above 1,500 rupees; below that it costs 99 rupees. Express delivery costs 249 rupees. Cargo bikes and other oversized items ship by freight for a flat 1,200 rupees.

## Tracking my order

When your order ships we email a tracking link. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. Freight shipments are tracked by container and may show as "in transit" for several days between depots; that is normal.

## My parcel says delivered but I don't have it

Check with neighbours and your building's reception, and look for a delivery photo in the tracking page. If you still cannot find it after 24 hours, contact support: we open an investigation with the courier and send a replacement or refund within 5 business days.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the address until the order is packed. After dispatch, ask support and we will try to redirect it with the courier; this is not always possible.

## Bulk and business orders

For orders of 10 or more bikes, business customers get freight pricing and a named account manager. Ask support for a quote.
