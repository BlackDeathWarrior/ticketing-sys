Account and sign-in help

Fictional account help for the Demo Store, used in TMS demos and tests.

Resetting your password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address on your account.

Two-step verification

Business accounts must use two-step verification. If you lose your phone, sign in with one of your backup codes and set up the new device from account settings. Support can reset two-step verification only after verifying your identity with the account owner.

Adding team members

Business account owners can invite up to 25 team members from account settings. Each member gets their own login; only owners can see invoices and payment methods.

Deleting your account

Ask support to delete your account. We remove your personal data within 30 days, except invoices, which we must keep for 8 years by law.
