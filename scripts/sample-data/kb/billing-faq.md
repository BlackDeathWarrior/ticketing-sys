# Billing and payments FAQ

Fictional billing help for the Demo Store, used in TMS demos and tests.

## I was charged twice

A second charge is usually a temporary authorisation that your bank releases within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges still show as settled after 5 business days, contact support with your order number and the last four digits of the card; we refund the duplicate within 2 business days of confirming it.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI, net banking, and pay-later for business accounts. Cash on delivery is available for orders under 5,000 rupees.

## Getting an invoice

Invoices are attached to the order confirmation email and can be downloaded from the Orders page. Business customers can add a GST number in account settings before ordering; we cannot change the GST number on an invoice after it is issued.

## Payment failed but money was deducted

If a payment fails but your account was debited, the bank reverses it automatically within 7 business days. The order is not placed, so place it again if you still want it.

## Subscriptions and plans

Service plans renew monthly on the day you signed up. Cancel from account settings at least one day before renewal to avoid the next charge; we do not refund partial months.
