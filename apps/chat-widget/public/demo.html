Demo store

This page stands in for a company website. The chat button in the corner is the TMS widget, added with one script tag: 

<script src="https://tms.example.com/widget/tms-chat.js"></script>
<script>TMSChat.init({ server: 'https://tms.example.com' });</script>

Messages you send here open a ticket in the TMS console (channel webchat). Replies from agents appear here live.

Prefer email? Submit a request in the help center (channel web_form); replies come by email.
